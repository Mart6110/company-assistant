# Manager Guide to Leave Approval and Absence

## Approving leave

Approve or decline leave requests within 3 working days. Decline only for a documented business reason, and suggest alternative dates when you do.
No more than 30% of a team should be on annual leave at the same time during the December holiday period.

## Long-term sickness

If a team member has been sick for more than 10 consecutive working days, contact the People Team to start the return-to-work process.
Do not ask employees about the medical nature of their illness.

## Absence patterns

If a team member has 4 or more separate sickness absences within 6 months, arrange a supportive check-in conversation and inform the People Team.
