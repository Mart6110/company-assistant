# Annual Leave Policy

## Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays.
Part-time employees receive leave pro-rated to their contracted hours: for example, an employee working 80% receives 20 days.

Employees who join or leave during the year receive leave pro-rated by the number of full months worked. Someone starting on 1 April accrues 9/12 of the annual entitlement for that year, which is 18.75 days, rounded up to the nearest half day (19 days).

## Requesting leave

Request leave through the assistant or the HR portal. Requests go to your line manager for approval.

- Leave of up to 3 working days: request at least 5 working days in advance.
- Leave of more than 3 working days: request at least 2 weeks in advance.
- Leave of more than 15 consecutive working days needs approval from both your manager and the People Team.

Managers respond within 3 working days. A request that has not been answered after 3 working days may be escalated to the People Team.

## Carry-over

You may carry over up to 5 unused days into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March; any carried-over days not used by then are forfeited.
Days above the 5-day limit are forfeited on 31 December unless the People Team approved an exception in writing, which is only granted when a business need prevented you from taking leave.

## Sick leave

Report sickness to your manager before 09:00 on the first day of absence. A doctor's note is required from the fourth consecutive day of sickness.
Sick leave does not reduce your annual leave balance. If you fall ill during annual leave and provide a doctor's note, those days are restored to your balance.

## Parental leave

Parental leave is covered by the separate Parental Leave Policy. Contact the People Team at least 8 weeks before the planned start date.
