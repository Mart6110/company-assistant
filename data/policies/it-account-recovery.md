# IT Support Runbook - Account Recovery

Internal: IT support staff only.

## Verifying identity

Before resetting any password or MFA device, verify the requester by a video call with their camera on and confirm their identity against their HR photo. Never reset credentials based on an email or chat request alone.

## MFA reset

Reset MFA in the identity admin console under Users > Security > Reset factors. Log the reset in the ticket with the verification method used.
Escalate any MFA reset request for an administrator account to the Security Team.
