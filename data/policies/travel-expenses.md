# Travel and Expense Policy

## Submitting expenses

Submit expenses in the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase, with an itemised receipt. Expenses submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed.
Expenses up to 250 EUR are approved by your manager. Expenses above 250 EUR also need approval from your department head.

## Travel

Book all business travel through the company travel portal.

- Flights: economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more.
- Trains are preferred over flights for journeys under 4 hours.
- Hotels: up to 180 EUR per night in most cities, up to 250 EUR per night in London, Paris, New York, Zurich and Tokyo.

## Meals and per diem

When travelling overnight, you may claim actual meal costs up to 60 EUR per day, or a flat per diem of 45 EUR per day without receipts. You cannot claim both for the same day.
Alcohol is not reimbursed, except at client events approved in advance by your department head.

## Company cards

Company credit cards are for business expenses only. Personal purchases on a company card must be reported to Finance within 5 working days and repaid.
