# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Hybrid schedule

Acme Corp works hybrid. Employees are expected in the office at least 2 days per week, with Tuesday as the shared team day. Teams may agree on a second fixed day.
Fully remote arrangements need written approval from your manager and the People Team and are reviewed every 12 months.

## Working from abroad

You may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year, provided you notify your manager and the People Team at least 2 weeks in advance.
Working abroad for longer than 20 days requires a tax and immigration review by the People Team before travel, because it can create tax obligations for you and the company.
Working from countries under trade sanctions is never allowed.

## Home office equipment

New employees receive a one-time home office allowance of 400 EUR for a chair, desk or monitor. Claim it as an expense within 6 months of your start date, with receipts.
Company laptops must not be shared with family members or used on public Wi-Fi without the company VPN.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Outside core hours you can arrange your day flexibly, as long as you meet your contracted hours.
