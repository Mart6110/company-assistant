# Compensation Bands and Salary Review Guidelines

Confidential: People Team and administrators only.

## Engineering salary bands (annual, EUR)

- Level E2: 52,000 to 64,000
- Level E3: 63,000 to 78,000
- Level E4: 77,000 to 95,000
- Level E5: 94,000 to 118,000

## Annual salary review

The annual review budget for 2026 is 4.5% of total payroll. Increases above 8% for one person need approval from the CFO.
Review outcomes are communicated by managers in March and take effect from 1 April.
